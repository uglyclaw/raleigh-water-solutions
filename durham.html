Water Quality in Durham

Durham's water supply comes from Lake Michie and Little River Reservoir. While treated to meet federal standards, many homeowners experience hard water, chlorine taste, and occasional sediment issues.

Our Durham customers typically see water hardness levels of 6-9 grains per gallon. This means scale buildup on fixtures, dry skin and hair, and reduced appliance efficiency.

We install whole-house systems that remove hardness, chlorine, and contaminants - giving you bottled-quality water from every tap.
