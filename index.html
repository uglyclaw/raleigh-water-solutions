FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. Here are some of the most common things our customers ask.

A whole home system treats water at the point where it enters your house. It typically includes a pre-filter to remove sediment, a carbon filter to remove chlorine and chemicals, and a water softener to remove hardness minerals. Every tap in your home delivers clean, filtered water.

Our systems are designed to remove chlorine, chloramines, sediment, rust, heavy metals like lead and mercury, pesticides, herbicides, and hardness minerals. Reverse osmosis systems also remove fluoride, nitrates, and other dissolved solids.

Pre-filters typically need replacement every 6-12 months. Carbon filters last about 12 months. RO membranes can last 2-3 years. We offer convenient filter replacement reminders and can handle the maintenance for you.

Our certified technician will test for hardness, chlorine, pH, total dissolved solids (TDS), iron, and other common contaminants found in Triangle water. We'll explain your results and recommend solutions based on your specific water quality.

Most installations are completed in 2-4 hours. We work around your schedule and make sure everything is working perfectly before we leave. No mess, no hassle.
